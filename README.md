# Fanny Pack

One small bar on your hip for everything you reach for between and during
fights: bandages, First Aid cures, healthstones, potions, elixirs, scrolls,
buff drinks, weapon stones and oils, food, drinks, buff food and camp gear.
Plain food and Well Fed food get separate buttons, so you never waste a feast
just to heal up. Made for **WoW Forever**.

## Why Fanny Pack

- **A bandage button** that bandages you (or your friendly target, if you
  like) and shows how long you're *Recently Bandaged*
- **Plain food and buff food apart**, with a soft glow on the buff food
  while you're not Well Fed
- **Stones, oils, poisons and mage imbue scrolls in one click**: no more
  clicking the item and then your weapon
- **Neat in combat**: food and drink step aside during a fight and the rest
  of the bar closes up
- **Picks the right item for you**: each button uses the best one you carry
  that you can use, scrolls in your class's order and stones that fit your
  weapon, and you can always choose another
- **Your own buttons** for any items, like raid potions or a trinket, and
  **Masque** skins to match your action bars

## The buttons

- **Healthstone**
- **Bandages**: bandages you, and shows how long you're *Recently Bandaged*,
  so you know when the next one is ready
- **Anti-venom**, **Poultice** and **Tourniquet**: First Aid's cures for
  poisons, diseases and bleeds, best (highest level cured) first. Out of
  combat each glows while you have something it cures. Anti-venom works in
  combat too;
  poultices and tourniquets can't be used then, so their buttons are off the
  bar in combat by default. Out of combat a cure is only used while you have
  something for it to cure, so it isn't wasted (Shift-click uses it anyway)
- **Health potion** and **Mana potion**
- **Rune**: Demonic Rune and Dark Rune, on their own cooldown apart from potions
- **Mana gem** (mages only): your conjured mana gem, also on its own cooldown,
  so you can keep it and a mana potion ready
- **Other potion**: every potion that isn't a health or mana potion, such as
  protection, Free Action, Swiftness and Rage potions
- **Elixir** and **Second elixir**: elixirs and flasks. The second one takes
  the best elixir the first doesn't use. They glow while their buff isn't on
  you
- **Scroll**: Scrolls of Agility, Intellect, Protection, Spirit, Stamina and
  Strength, used on you. It takes the stats in your class's order (Strength
  first for a warrior, Agility for a hunter or rogue, Intellect for casters);
  sort them yourself under **Scroll order** on the options' Reminders page.
  It doesn't glow for a scroll a class buff already covers, like Stamina
  under Power Word: Fortitude. Mage scrolls stay off it (see below)
- **Familiar** (mages only): summons your best familiar scroll, Cat, Frog or
  Rat, as soon as your level allows it. It glows gold while no familiar is
  out, and silver near the end of its hour
- **Other buffs**: anything else that raises a stat for a while, like
  Rumsey Rum Black Label, juju and buff drinks from fishing. It glows while
  its buff isn't on you
- **Weapon stone or oil**: sharpening stones, weightstones, wizard oils and
  mana oils. Click to put one on your main hand weapon, Shift-click for your
  off-hand. It only uses stones that fit the weapon: sharpening stones on
  axes, swords, daggers and polearms, weightstones on maces and staves, so a
  sword and a mace each get their own. Casters get oils first (mages and
  warlocks wizard oil, priests and druids mana oil), the rest stones; change
  it under **Weapon stone or oil** on the options' Items page. It glows gold
  while a weapon in your hands has nothing on it, and silver when it's
  running out. Mages also get their **imbue scrolls** here, each only for the
  weapon it's made for (a staff, dagger or sword); choose **Imbues** to use
  them before oils. Mage scrolls without a button (Cryoblast, Minor
  Evocation and the like) stay off the bar, and other classes never get
  mage scrolls at all
- **Main-hand poison** and **Off-hand poison** (rogues only): one click puts
  the poison on that hand. Each button has its own poison, and glows while
  its hand has nothing on it
- **Food**: ordinary food that only restores health
- **Drink**: water and other drinks that restore mana
- **Eat & drink**: eats and drinks in one click, with the items of your Food
  and Drink buttons
- **Buff food**: food that makes you *Well Fed*, kept apart from your
  everyday food. It glows gold while you're not Well Fed, and silver when
  Well Fed is running out
- **Camp gear**: click it and a row opens with all your camp gear (your
  sleeping bag, tents, chairs and other camp objects), each with its
  cooldown; click one to use it. Right-click it to hide camp gear you don't
  want in the row. It's off the bar in combat by default, and
  in dungeons, raids, cities and inns, where you can't camp

Turn on **Use on your target** to put bandages, cures and scrolls on a friendly
target instead; with no target, or an enemy targeted, they still go on you.

Turn on **Hide while on** for a reminder and its buttons stay off the bar
while the buff is on you, coming back when it's gone or running out. For the
cures, it keeps them off the bar until you have a poison, disease or bleed.

**Make the glow easy to see** on the options' Reminders page: a soft halo,
a border tight around the button (clear on any ground), both, or the action
bars' sparkling proc glow; how strongly it shows (50 to 200%); and whether it
pulses. A preview shows the result as you change it.

The silver glow starts 2 minutes before a buff or weapon stone runs out. On
the options' Reminders page you can set it separately for Well Fed (up to 15
minutes), elixirs (60), scrolls (30), other buffs (60), familiars (60, mages
only) and weapons (30), or 0 for none.

Each button uses the best item you carry that you can use: the food, drink,
bandage or potion that restores the most, otherwise the highest level, and
conjured food and water before anything you'd keep. It shows how
many you have left and its cooldown. **Right-click** a button to pick a
different item from your bags, to use up low-level items first instead, and to choose when its count turns **red** as
you run low (5 bandages, 3 potions and so on, by default). All the low-stock
limits are also listed together on the options' Reminders page, where you
can turn the warning off altogether.

**Macros for your own action bars**: on the options' Items page, add a macro
for any button. Fanny Pack keeps it using the item that button would use, so
you can drag it onto Bartender, Dominos or the game's bars and still get the
best food, potion or bandage.

**Make your own buttons** on the options' Custom page, for any items you
like: raid potions, Holy Water, a trinket, a fishing pole. Name the button
and add items by dragging them from your bags, Shift-clicking them or typing
a name or ID; you can also drop an item from your bags straight onto a
custom button on the bar. It uses the first item in its list that you carry
(drag the items to sort them), and an item on a custom button leaves its
usual button, so you can give Free Action Potions a button of their own.
For items that give you a buff, like juju or a flask, turn on **Glow while
its buff is missing** on the button's card.
Custom buttons are shared by all your characters; each profile turns them on
or off and sorts them on the Buttons page, like the other buttons. Their key
bindings are set by hovering (`/fannypack bind`), not in the game's Key
Bindings.

**Hide items** you don't want on any button, like a flask you're saving
for a raid: click the small **x** next to it in a button's right-click
list. No button uses it from then on. Hidden items are listed under
**Hidden** at the bottom of that list and on the options' Items page,
where you can show them again.

Buttons for things you don't carry are hidden (or faded, if you prefer).
Warriors and rogues don't get the drink and mana potion buttons by default.

## In combat

- Every button works in combat, and so do their **hotkeys**: click **Bind
  keys** on the Buttons page (or type `/fannypack bind`), hover a button and
  press a key. The key shows in the button's corner. They're also in the
  game's Key Bindings, under Fanny Pack
- **Choose which buttons show in and out of combat.** Food, drink and buff
  food hide during a fight by default, since you can't eat or drink then; a
  button can also show only in fights. The bar closes up around whatever is
  hidden, and switches back when the fight is over
- The reminder glows wait until the fight is over (the game keeps your buffs
  and debuffs hidden from addons during a fight)
- If your bags change mid-fight, the bar catches up as soon as combat ends
  (the game doesn't let addons change their buttons during combat)

## Setting up the bar

Type `/fannypack` (or click the minimap button) to open the options.

**Where it sits**
- **Above your player frame** by default, lined up with its right end, clear
  of the pet frame and the cast bar. If your player frame is at the top of
  the screen, it goes below it instead
- **Attached to the player frame**: it moves along when you move the player
  frame in Edit Mode, also after you've dragged it. Turn this off to pin it
  to the screen
- **Unlock** it to drag it anywhere, and **reset** it to put it back
- **Exact position**: type X and Y on the Bar page to place it to the point,
  from the player frame while attached, otherwise from the middle of the screen.
  Or nudge the unlocked bar with the small arrows around it, or your arrow keys
  while the mouse is over it: one pixel at a time, ten with Shift

**How it's laid out**
- **Sort the buttons** in any order by dragging them in the options list, and
  turn each one on or off
- Let the bar **grow** right, left, down or up from its first button. The
  first button always stays put: turning buttons on or off, or hiding them
  in combat, never moves it
- **Split it into rows**: choose how many buttons go in a row, and whether
  new rows go above or below (or left or right of a bar growing up or down)

**How it looks**
- **Shape**: square, rounded or round
- **Border**: none, or a thin silver or gold line
- **Button size** and **spacing**
- **Masque**: with the Masque addon installed, pick a skin for the "Fanny
  Pack" group in Masque's options (`/msq`) and the buttons match your action
  bars. The skin then decides the shape and border
- **Fade out of combat**: the bar disappears outside combat and comes back
  when you hover over it or a fight starts. A glowing reminder stays visible
- Each **reminder** glow can be turned off on the Reminders page

**Profiles**
- Keep a whole bar setup per role, say one for tanking and one for healing:
  which buttons show, their order, the layout, the look, the low-stock
  limits, the scroll order and stones, oils or imbues first
- Each character uses a profile (Default to start). Switch, create, copy,
  reset and delete them in the options, or type `/fannypack profile <name>`
  (handy in a macro)

The options are split into pages (Buttons, Custom, Items, Bar, Look,
Reminders and Profiles), and the window can be resized to fit small screens. After an
update with new features, a line in chat and a card at the top of the
options say what's new (the chat line can be turned off). On your first
login, a short tour beside the bar shows you around; `/fannypack tour` plays
it again.

## Commands

| Command | |
|---|---|
| `/fannypack` | Open the options |
| `/fannypack lock` | Lock or unlock the bar |
| `/fannypack reset` | Put the bar back above your player frame |
| `/fannypack profile [name]` | Show or switch this character's profile |
| `/fannypack minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/fannypack tour` | A short tour of the bar |
| `/fannypack bind` | Bind keys by hovering the buttons |
| `/fannypack debug` | A report to copy into bug reports: the items in your bags and which button each one is on |

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue on GitHub. Please include what you did and what happened, the
Fanny Pack version (shown next to the title in the options, `/fannypack`)
and any error message you got. If an item is on the wrong button, or on
none, type `/fannypack debug`, press Ctrl+C in the window that opens (the
text is already selected) and paste it into your report.
